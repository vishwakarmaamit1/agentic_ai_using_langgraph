Table Schema:

1. Column Name: product_group_id
   Description: A unique identifier assigned to each distinct product group within the retail system. It serves as the primary key for this dimension table.
   Business Context: Essential for joining with fact tables (e.g., sales, inventory) to analyze performance, trends, and profitability at the product group level. It allows analysts to segment sales data by product group, understand which groups are performing well, and track inventory levels for specific product categories.

2. Column Name: product_group_name
   Description: The descriptive name given to a product group, providing a human-readable label for identification.
   Business Context: Provides a user-friendly label for reports, dashboards, and analytical tools, making it easy for business users to understand and interpret data related to product groups (e.g., "Electronics," "Apparel," "Home Goods"). It's crucial for clear communication and presentation of insights.

3. Column Name: business_group_id
   Description: A unique identifier for a broader business segment or category to which the product group belongs, allowing for hierarchical organization.
   Business Context: Facilitates hierarchical analysis, allowing retailers to aggregate product group performance up to a broader business unit or department level (e.g., "General Merchandise," "Food & Beverage"). This helps in understanding the performance of larger segments of the business, resource allocation, and strategic decision-making across different business divisions.

4. Column Name: description
   Description: A comprehensive textual explanation or definition providing additional details about the product group.
   Business Context: Offers supplementary information about the product group, such as its scope, typical products included, or specific characteristics. This is valuable for new analysts, training, or when a product group's name isn't fully self-explanatory, ensuring a deeper understanding of the data being analyzed.
