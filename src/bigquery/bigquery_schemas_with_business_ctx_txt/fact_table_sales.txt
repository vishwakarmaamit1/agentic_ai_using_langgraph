Table Schema:

1. Column Name: sale_id
   Description: A unique identifier assigned to each individual sales transaction. This serves as the primary key for the fact table.
   Business Context: Used to uniquely identify and track every single sale event, enabling granular analysis, auditing, and joining with other sales-related data. Essential for understanding individual transaction details and for data integrity.

2. Column Name: product_id
   Description: An identifier that uniquely references a specific product involved in the sale.
   Business Context: Links the sales record to the `dim_product` table, providing detailed information about the product sold (e.g., name, category, brand, SKU). Critical for analyzing product performance, sales by category, and inventory management.

3. Column Name: vendor_id
   Description: An identifier that uniquely references the vendor or supplier of the product sold.
   Business Context: Links the sales record to the `dim_vendor` table, allowing analysis of sales performance attributed to specific vendors. Useful for vendor management, procurement optimization, and evaluating supplier impact on sales.

4. Column Name: location_id
   Description: An identifier that uniquely references the physical or virtual location where the sale transaction occurred.
   Business Context: Links the sales record to the `dim_location` table (e.g., store, warehouse, region, website). Enables geographical or store-specific sales analysis, understanding regional performance, and optimizing store operations or online fulfillment.

5. Column Name: channel_id
   Description: An identifier that uniquely references the sales channel through which the transaction was completed.
   Business Context: Links the sales record to the `dim_channel` table (e.g., online, in-store, mobile app, call center). Allows for analysis of sales performance across different channels, crucial for multi-channel strategy, channel optimization, and customer journey mapping.

6. Column Name: date_id
   Description: The calendar date on which the sales transaction was recorded.
   Business Context: Used for time-based analysis, enabling aggregation of sales data by day, week, month, quarter, or year. Essential for trend analysis, seasonality detection, period-over-period comparisons, and financial reporting.

7. Column Name: sale_timestamp
   Description: The exact date and time when the sales transaction was completed and recorded in the system.
   Business Context: Provides a precise timestamp for the sale, enabling granular analysis of sales patterns within a day (e.g., peak hours), real-time operational insights, and detailed event sequencing. Useful for fraud detection and understanding transaction flow.

8. Column Name: quantity_sold
   Description: The number of units of the product sold in this specific transaction line item.
   Business Context: Represents the volume of products sold. Used to calculate total units sold, average units per transaction, inventory depletion rates, and is a key metric for demand forecasting and inventory planning.

9. Column Name: total_sales
   Description: The gross monetary value generated from this specific sale line item before any discounts, returns, or taxes are applied.
   Business Context: Represents the total revenue generated from the sale before adjustments. Used for calculating gross revenue, tracking sales targets, and understanding the overall top-line performance of products or channels.

10. Column Name: margin
   Description: The profit margin for this specific sale line item, typically calculated as (selling price - cost of goods sold).
   Business Context: Indicates the profitability of each individual sale. Essential for understanding product profitability, evaluating pricing strategies, assessing the financial health of product categories, and optimizing product assortment.

11. Column Name: discount
   Description: The monetary value of any discount applied to this specific sale line item.
   Business Context: Tracks the financial impact of promotional activities and discounts on sales revenue and profitability. Used to analyze the effectiveness and cost of promotions, and to understand customer price sensitivity.

12. Column Name: net_amount
   Description: The final monetary amount received from the customer for this sale line item after applying all discounts but before taxes.
   Business Context: Represents the actual revenue recognized from the sale after all adjustments. This is a critical metric for calculating net revenue, understanding the true financial intake from each transaction, and for financial reconciliation.

13. Column Name: created_at
   Description: The timestamp indicating when this specific sales record was first created and ingested into the data system.
   Business Context: Used for data lineage, auditing, and understanding the freshness of data. Helps in tracking data ingestion processes, identifying potential delays, and ensuring data quality and governance.

14. Column Name: updated_at
   Description: The timestamp indicating when this specific sales record was last modified or updated in the system.
   Business Context: Used for data auditing, tracking changes to sales records (e.g., post-sale adjustments, returns, corrections), and ensuring data accuracy and reconciliation. Important for maintaining data integrity and understanding data evolution.
