Table Schema:

1. Column Name: date_id
   Description: The unique identifier for a specific calendar date, serving as the primary key for the date dimension.
   Business Context: Used to join with fact tables (e.g., sales, inventory, customer transactions) to enable precise time-based filtering, aggregation, and analysis of retail data at a daily granularity. It is fundamental for tracking daily performance, identifying short-term trends, and comparing metrics across specific dates.

2. Column Name: day
   Description: The day of the month, represented as an integer from 1 to 31.
   Business Context: Useful for analyzing daily patterns within a month, such as identifying peak sales days, understanding customer behavior on specific days of the month (e.g., first of the month, end-of-month sales), or evaluating the impact of promotions tied to specific dates.

3. Column Name: month
   Description: The calendar month number, represented as an integer from 1 (January) to 12 (December).
   Business Context: Enables monthly aggregation and comparison of key retail metrics like sales revenue, transaction count, or inventory turnover. It is vital for tracking monthly performance against targets, identifying seasonal trends, and preparing monthly business reports and forecasts.

4. Column Name: month_name
   Description: The full name of the calendar month (e.g., 'January', 'February').
   Business Context: Provides a user-friendly and descriptive representation of the month for reporting and visualization purposes. It simplifies report interpretation for business users who prefer descriptive names over numerical codes when analyzing monthly trends or creating dashboards.

5. Column Name: quarter
   Description: The calendar quarter number, represented as an integer from 1 to 4.
   Business Context: Facilitates quarterly business reviews and performance analysis. Retailers use this field to evaluate the success of strategic initiatives, compare performance year-over-year by quarter, and align reporting with financial cycles.

6. Column Name: year
   Description: The four-digit calendar year.
   Business Context: Essential for year-over-year comparisons, long-term trend analysis, and annual performance reporting. It allows retailers to track growth, identify multi-year patterns, assess the impact of long-term strategies, and set annual budgets and goals.

7. Column Name: week_of_year
   Description: The week number within the year, typically ranging from 1 to 52 or 53, often following ISO 8601 standards.
   Business Context: Used for weekly performance tracking, comparing sales or inventory levels week-over-week, and identifying weekly trends. This is particularly important for operational planning, staffing optimization, and scheduling promotional campaigns in retail.

8. Column Name: is_weekend
   Description: A boolean flag indicating whether the `date_id` falls on a weekend (TRUE) or a weekday (FALSE).
   Business Context: Crucial for analyzing differences in retail activity between weekdays and weekends. Retailers often observe distinct sales patterns, customer traffic, and staffing requirements on weekends, making this field valuable for optimizing store operations, marketing efforts, and promotional timing.

9. Column Name: year_month
   Description: A concatenated string representing the year and month, typically in 'YYYY-MM' format (e.g., '2023-01').
   Business Context: Provides a convenient and unambiguous way to group and sort data chronologically by month across multiple years. It is frequently used in reports and dashboards for monthly trend analysis where the specific year is also important, allowing for clear identification of each monthly period.

10. Column Name: year_quarter
   Description: A concatenated string representing the year and quarter, typically in 'YYYY-Q[Q]' format (e.g., '2023-Q1').
   Business Context: Similar to `year_month`, this field simplifies grouping and sorting data by quarter across different years. It is used for quarterly performance reviews, comparing quarterly results year-over-year, and visualizing long-term quarterly trends in a clear, unambiguous format for business stakeholders.
